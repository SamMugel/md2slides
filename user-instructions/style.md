## Overview
This Skill provides Multiverse Computing's official brand guidelines for creating consistent, professional materials. When creating presentations, documents, or marketing materials, apply these standards to ensure all outputs match Multiverse's visual identity. Claude should reference these guidelines whenever creating external-facing materials or documents that represent Multiverse Computing.

## Brand Colors

Our official brand colors are:
- Red (#FF0000) — likely the prominent brand accent (e.g., in logo or key elements)
- Catskill White (#F8FAFC) — light background or neutral surface
- Woodsmoke (#111417) — dark text or background contrast

## Typography

Headers: Montserrat Bold
Body text: Open Sans Regular
Size guidelines:
- H1: 32pt
- H2: 24pt
- Body: 11pt

## Logo Usage

Always use the full-color logo on light backgrounds. Use the white logo on dark backgrounds. Maintain minimum spacing of 0.5 inches around the logo.

## When to Apply

Apply these guidelines whenever creating:
- PowerPoint presentations
- Word documents for external sharing
- Marketing materials
- Reports for clients
